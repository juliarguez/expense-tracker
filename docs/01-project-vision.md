# BALTRA - Project Vision

## Overview

BALTRA is a web application created to help users keep track of their personal expenses in a simple and organized way.

The main idea behind the project is to make it easier to understand where money is being spent. Instead of only storing expenses, BALTRA also provides summaries and charts that help users visualize their spending habits.

---

## Problem Statement

Many people do not have a clear idea of how much money they spend every month or what they spend it on. Although there are many finance applications available, some of them include too many features and can feel difficult to use. On the other hand, spreadsheets require manual work and are not always practical for everyday use.

BALTRA aims to provide a simple and intuitive solution that allows users to record their expenses and better understand their financial habits.

---

## Target Users

BALTRA is mainly intended for:

- University students
- Young professionals
- Freelancers
- Anyone who wants to have better control over their personal finances

---

## Project Goals

The main goals of BALTRA are:

- Make expense tracking simple.
- Help users understand their spending habits.
- Provide useful summaries and charts.
- Offer a clean and easy-to-use interface.
- Build a complete and reliable web application.

---

## MVP Features

The first version of BALTRA will include:

- User registration and login
- Expense management
- Custom categories
- Dashboard with spending summaries
- Expense filters
- Basic charts
- CSV export

The objective is to build a stable and well-organized application before adding more advanced features.

---

## Future Improvements

Some ideas for future versions of BALTRA include:

- Budget management
- Savings goals
- Smart spending recommendations
- AI-powered insights
- Mobile application
- Bank account synchronization

These features are outside the scope of version 1.0 but could be implemented in future updates.
